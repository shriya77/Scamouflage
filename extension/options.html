      About & privacy

      • Every check happens inside your browser. Hostnames are hashed (SHA-256) and compared with the downloaded list; the sites you visit are never sent anywhere.
      • The list is signed with Ed25519 and is only used if the signature checks out.
      • A flagged site is a likely scam, not a confirmed one. A site that isn't on the list isn't proven safe.
