Every alert is signed

Each scam alert is signed with Ed25519 on IBM's CPACF crypto chip. Anyone can check it with our public key, so a forged or edited alert is caught instantly.

🕶️

We never see your browsing

The extension downloads a signed list of hashed scam domains and checks each site on your own device. No website you visit is ever sent to us.

🧪

We never open scam sites

The AI works only from public certificate data. Suspected scam domains are shown as plain text, never as clickable links.

      Free Chrome extension

      Get warned before
      you pay a fake toll.

      If you tap a link in a "you owe a toll" or "your package is held" text, Scamouflage shows a full-page warning before you can pay, and tells you why the site looks fake. It checks every site privately, on your own device.

      Download the extension
      Chrome extension. Free, no account needed.
      1

      It keeps a signed list

      Every 30 minutes the extension downloads our list of flagged domains and checks its Ed25519 signature. A tampered or older list is rejected.

      2

      It checks privately

      For each site you open, it hashes the domain and looks it up in the list stored in your browser. Nothing you browse leaves your device.

      3

      You get a clear warning

      On a match you see a full-page warning with the score and the reasons, then choose Go back or Continue anyway.

      Install it in Chrome

      1. Download the extension folder from our project.
      2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
      3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
      4. Pin Scamouflage to your toolbar. You're protected.

      Before a Chrome Web Store listing, Chrome installs extensions this way. Demo mode flags a harmless local test page, so the demo never opens a real scam site.
